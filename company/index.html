Meet Mechasys

Digital design. Human ambition.

Our spatial-computing laser projection solution turns designs into full-scale digital visual models, enabling people, technology and data to work together for more precise and efficient execution.

We bring hardware, software and Customer Success support together so construction teams can prepare the work, see selected design elements on site and learn how to use the solution in their operations.

Headquartered in Montréal, with offices in France and the U.S. and an international distribution network, Mechasys develops its own proprietary projection technology and connected software.

Our mission

To transform how precisely and efficiently the world builds through projected reality, creating a seamless interface that connects design, data, people and execution in the field.

Our vision

To become the operating system for projected reality, transforming real-time understanding of physical environments into intelligent interfaces that guide work and enable people and autonomous systems to collaborate seamlessly.
