Four stages from coordinated model to live jobsite.

Follow one focused work package from office preparation through wireless 1:1 projection and verified field feedback.

01

Layout Cloud™ — Prepare only what the field needs.

Use Mechasys’ Layout Cloud™ to review DWG and DXF files exported from BIM or design software. Select the required scope, confirm jobsite control points, and send the focused work package straight to Layout Field.

  • DWG & DXF work packages
  • Scope isolation
  • Control point verification
02

Layout Field & XR Projector — See the plan at 1:1 scale.

Controlled wirelessly through Layout Field on a rugged tablet, the XR Projector displays selected lines, drill points, anchor locations, and openings across supported floors, walls, and ceilings. Operators can switch views and check tolerances while they work.

  • Wireless rugged tablet
  • Multi-surface projection
  • Instant layer toggling
Explore the XR Projector
03

Full-Scale Projection — Bring the plan into a shared view, where the work happens.

Selected lines, anchors, and openings appear at true 1:1 scale for every trade in the room. Relevant field execution information can be brought back into the project workflow, helping teams carry what they learn into the next work package, phase, or project.

  • Shared trade visibility
  • Floors, walls & ceilings
  • Execution feedback loop
04

Customer Success Experts — Put the workflow in your team’s hands.

Mechasys Customer Success experts support file and site readiness, instrument setup, hands-on operator training, and Field Validation. Your team can evaluate the solution under real project conditions before determining the right deployment path.

  • File & site readiness
  • Instrument setup & training
  • Live Field Validation
